Will the app be published under my own company name on Play Store and App Store?

Yes, absolutely! The mobile apps are published directly under your own Google Play Developer and Apple Developer accounts using your official brand name and logo.

How long does it take to develop and launch the app?

Typically, a standard restaurant app or web ordering system is fully developed and ready for live demonstration within 7 to 10 working days.

Where do I receive customer orders?

Orders appear instantly on your web-based admin dashboard with live audio alerts, and complete order details can also be dispatched directly to your WhatsApp.

Do I have to pay any monthly commission on my sales?

No, not at all! This is a 0% commission system. You keep 100% of your sales revenue and profits without any third-party cuts.
